## A little more context

My work has included indoor positioning with BLE, GPS, and IMU signals, recommendation data pipelines, insurance analytics, and Web3 community infrastructure. I enjoy projects where the interesting part is making several imperfect pieces work together.

I received an **MSc in Big Data Technology from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology** (GGA 3.76) and a **BSc in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from South China University of Technology** (GPA: 3.63/4.00, top 10%).
