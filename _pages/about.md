# Data, systems, and useful ideas

I am **Keyan Lu**, a data and software engineer with a background in mathematics and big data technology.

My work sits at the intersection of data analysis, machine learning, and practical software systems. I have worked on indoor positioning with BLE, GPS, and IMU signals; recommendation data pipelines; insurance analytics; and Web3 community infrastructure.

I received an **MSc in Big Data Technology from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology** (GGA 3.76) and a **BSc in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from South China University of Technology** (GPA 3.63/4.00, top 10%).

This site collects selected work, technical notes, and a current CV. It is built to grow with each new project.
